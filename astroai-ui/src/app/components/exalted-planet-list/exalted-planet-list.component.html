What is an Exalted Planet?

In Vedic astrology, each planet has a specific zodiac sign where its energy flows most freely and powerfully — called its Uccha Rashi (sign of exaltation). When a planet is placed in this sign at birth, it is said to be exalted (Uccha) and capable of bestowing its finest qualities with full strength and grace. An exalted planet is one of the most powerful indicators of exceptional talent, achievement and divine favour in a birth chart.

Why Exalted Planets Are Powerful

Classical Vedic texts like Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra describe a planet in its Uccha Rashi as operating with full, unobstructed vitality. Unlike planets placed in challenging signs, an exalted planet channels its full spectrum of qualities — whether it is the Sun's authority, the Moon's emotional peace, Jupiter's wisdom or Venus's love — without resistance or distortion.
