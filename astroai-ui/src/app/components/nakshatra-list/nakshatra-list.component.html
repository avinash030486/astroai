What is a Nakshatra?

The Nakshatra system divides the 360° zodiac into 27 equal parts of 13°20′ each, corresponding to the Moon's daily movement through the sky. Each Nakshatra is a lunar mansion with its own presiding deity, ruling planet, symbol and unique energy field. While the Sun sign (Rashi) describes your outer personality, the Nakshatra of your natal Moon reveals the texture of your mind, emotional instincts and subconscious patterns — the most intimate layer of your Vedic chart.

Finding your Janma Nakshatra (birth star) and understanding its deity, qualities and remedies gives you a practical map for navigating relationships, career timing and spiritual practice in alignment with your cosmic nature.
