✦ Quick Overview
This summary provides key points from our privacy notice. Use the table of contents to navigate to any section, or read the full policy below. For questions, contact privacy@vedicastro.app.
What personal information do we process?

When you register for and use VedicAstro, we collect information you provide directly — including your name, email address, date of birth, birth time, birth location, and current location — to generate your personalised Vedic birth chart, Dasha timeline, Panchang, and related astrological services.

AI Jyotishi consultations

Conversations you have with our AI Jyotishi and astrologer bots are processed transiently to generate your response and are not stored or retained by VedicAstro Inc after the session ends.

Do we sell your data?

No. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party for commercial or marketing purposes, ever.

Your rights

Depending on where you reside, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information. Contact us at privacy@vedicastro.app to exercise any of these rights.

01

What Information Do We Collect?

Personal information you provide to us

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when you register for an account, set up your birth chart profile, make a purchase, or otherwise contact us. The personal information we collect includes:

  • Full name — used to personalise your experience and astrological readings
  • Email address — used for account authentication, weekly updates, and service communications
  • Date of birth — essential for calculating your Vedic birth chart, Nakshatras, and Dasha periods
  • Birth time — required for accurate Ascendant (Lagna) and house placement calculations
  • Birth location (city, country) — used to determine the precise planetary positions at the time of your birth
  • Current location — used to generate location-relevant Panchang data, Muhurats, and daily horoscopes

All personal information you provide must be truthful, complete, and accurate.

Information automatically collected

When you access or use VedicAstro, certain technical and usage data is automatically collected to operate, secure, and improve the service.

Log and Usage Data

Service-related diagnostic and performance data our servers automatically record, including your IP address, device information, browser or app version, activity timestamps, and actions taken within the app.

Device Data

Information about the device you use to access VedicAstro, including device type, operating system, unique device identifiers, and crash or error logs.

Location Data

We collect your current location to deliver location-accurate Panchang information, Muhurat windows, and regional horoscope content. You may opt out by disabling location permissions in your device settings.

Payment information

Payments are processed exclusively by our PCI-compliant third-party payment processor. We do not collect, store, or have access to your full credit card or banking details.

02

How Do We Process Your Information?

We process your personal information for the following purposes:

  • Account creation & authentication — to register you, verify your identity, and maintain your account.
  • Delivery of astrological services — to generate your Vedic birth chart, Nakshatra profile, Dasha timeline, daily horoscope, Panchang, Yoga report, and Muhurat windows.
  • AI Jyotishi interactions — to pass your birth data and question to our AI astrologer model. Conversation content is not retained after the session.
  • Weekly email updates — to send your personalised horoscope, Panchang summary, and Yoga updates by email, where you have opted in.
  • Billing & payment processing — to process your subscription or credit purchase via our payment provider.
  • Customer support — to respond to your enquiries, resolve issues, and improve your experience.
  • Security & fraud prevention — to monitor for suspicious activity, protect accounts, and enforce our Terms.
  • Product improvement — to analyse aggregated and anonymised usage patterns.
  • Legal compliance — to meet our obligations under applicable law.
03

AI Consultations & the Jyotishi Bot

✦ Important — Conversation Data
VedicAstro does not store the content of your conversations with our AI Jyotishi, astrologer bots, or any other AI-powered consultation features after your session ends.
No persistent conversation storage

Conversations with our AI Jyotishi are processed transiently in real-time. VedicAstro Inc does not log, store, or retain the content of these conversations after the session concludes.

Data passed to AI models

Relevant elements of your birth profile and your current query are passed to the underlying AI model during the active session only. This context is not retained by VedicAstro after the response is delivered.

Third-party AI infrastructure

Our AI features are powered by third-party providers bound by data processing agreements that prohibit any secondary use of your data.

Disclaimer regarding AI-generated readings

Our AI Jyotishi provides astrological guidance for entertainment, reflection, and educational purposes only. It is not a substitute for professional medical, legal, financial, or psychological advice.

04

Legal Bases for Processing

  • Contractual necessity — processing your birth data, name, email, and location is necessary to provide the astrological services you signed up for.
  • Consent — for optional communications such as weekly email updates. You may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legitimate interests — for fraud prevention, security monitoring, product improvement, and analytics.
  • Legal obligation — to comply with Canadian privacy legislation (PIPEDA / Quebec Law 25) and other regulatory obligations.
05

Who Do We Share Your Data With?

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the following categories of service providers:

AI infrastructure providers

Your birth data and queries are passed to our AI model providers under data processing agreements prohibiting secondary use.

Payment processors

Payment information is handled by PCI-compliant processors (such as Stripe). We share only the minimum information required to complete transactions.

Email delivery providers

Your email address is shared with our transactional email provider for account-related communications and, where opted in, weekly horoscope updates.

Analytics providers

We use analytics tools that receive anonymised and aggregated usage data only — no name, birth data, or personally identifiable information.

Legal and regulatory authorities

We may disclose personal information where required by law, court order, or to protect the safety of any person or prevent fraud.

06

Cookies & Tracking Technologies

  • Essential cookies — necessary to keep you logged in and maintain your session securely.
  • Analytics cookies — anonymised, aggregated data on how users interact with the app. No personal identifiers are included.

We do not use advertising cookies, third-party tracking pixels, or cross-site behavioural tracking of any kind.

07

How Long Do We Keep Your Information?

  • Account data — retained for the life of your account.
  • AI consultation content — not retained after session end.
  • Transaction records — retained for a minimum of 7 years to meet Canadian financial record-keeping obligations.
  • Technical logs — retained for a rolling period of up to 90 days.
  • Anonymised analytics data — may be retained indefinitely.

When you delete your account, we will delete or anonymise your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

08

How Do We Keep Your Information Safe?

  • Encryption of data in transit using TLS
  • Encryption of sensitive data at rest
  • Access controls and role-based permissions
  • Regular security reviews and vulnerability assessments
  • Incident response procedures for breach notification

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. If you suspect your account has been compromised, contact us immediately at privacy@vedicastro.app.

09

Information from Minors

VedicAstro is not directed at individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe your child has provided us personal information, contact us at privacy@vedicastro.app and we will promptly delete it.
